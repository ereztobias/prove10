Prove10 privacy policy

Privacy policy

One email address, so we can send you one email when there is something to use. That is the entire purpose. There is no newsletter, no second list, and nothing is shared with anyone.

Who is asking

prove10, a business operated from Israel that is not yet registered as a company. When it is, this page will name the entity and its address. Until then the person behind it answers at hello@prove10.com, and that is the address to use for anything on this page.

What is kept

  • Your email address.
  • The time you submitted it and the time you confirmed it.
  • The two-letter country the request came from, as reported by our network provider. Not a city, not a location.
  • A salted SHA-256 hash of your IP address — never the address itself. It exists only to stop one machine submitting hundreds of times, and cannot be turned back into an IP.
  • The exact sentence you agreed to, so that what you consented to is a matter of record rather than of memory.

What is not kept

No name, no company, no job title. No cookies — this site sets none at all. No analytics, no advertising pixels, no tracking of any kind, and no profile built about you. Typefaces come from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address as a consequence of serving them; nothing else on these pages contacts a third party.

Why it is lawful

Consent, under Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR, given twice: once when you submit the form, once when you click the link in the email we send you. An address that has not been confirmed is never sent anything. Consent is the only basis used, and it is specific to the single email described above.

How long

Until you unsubscribe, or until that one email has gone out and twelve months have passed — whichever comes first. Then the row is deleted. If you unsubscribe, the row is kept marked as unsubscribed instead of erased, so that a later import cannot quietly put you back. Ask for erasure and it goes entirely.

Who else touches it

  • Cloudflare — stores the list and runs the code that receives it.
  • Resend — sends the mail, and therefore sees your address.

Nobody else. The list is not sold, rented, shared or used to find you anywhere else.

Getting out, or getting it back

Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe link, and it works immediately. You can also ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, delete it, or hand it over in a portable form, and withdraw your consent at any time for any reason. One line to hello@prove10.com is enough — no form, no account, no explanation needed.

If you are in the EU or the UK and think this has been handled badly, you may complain to your national data protection authority.
