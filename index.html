AI product suite

One core. A suite of AI products on top of it.

prove10 builds and runs its own software. Each product solves a narrow problem completely. They sit on the same core, which is why the next one takes weeks.

What the products have in common

Each one is software where the model does real work and carries real responsibility, not a chat box bolted onto a screen that already existed. Each stays small enough for one person to hold in their head end to end. None of them is a platform waiting for you to configure it.

How the next one ships in weeks

  1. Narrow the problem

    One user, one job, one outcome. Most of the speed comes from what gets left out.

  2. Build on the core

    Accounts, data handling, model access and deployment already exist and are already proven. A new product inherits them on day one.

  3. Ship, then measure

    It goes live early. What people actually do with it decides the next version, instead of what we assumed in week one.

Why weeks and not quarters

Nothing here is a trick of estimation. The scope is smaller, the core is already built, the stack is deliberately boring, and the person designing a product is the person shipping it. Ten times faster is mostly ten times less waiting.

What proven means here
